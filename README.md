📊 Sales Performance Analysis – Power BI

## Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard created to analyze sales performance and generate business insights.

## Key KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Total Customers

## Dashboard Analysis

- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Top 10 Salespersons
- Sales by Payment Method
- Sales by City
- Profit by Category

## Interactive Filters

- Order Date
- Category
- Region

## Tool Used

*Microsoft Power BI*
